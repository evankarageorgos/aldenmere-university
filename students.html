Campus Life

More Than
a Degree

From world-class sports facilities to a thriving arts scene, life at Aldenmere is designed to challenge and delight at every turn.

Accommodation

Guaranteed accommodation for all first-year and international students across eight residential colleges. Single en-suite and catered options available. 98% of students live within 10 minutes of their faculty building.

Sport & Fitness

Over 50 sports clubs from rowing and rugby to fencing and water polo. The Aldenmere Sports Centre features an Olympic pool, climbing wall, and strength & conditioning suite open 06:00–23:00 daily.

Arts & Culture

The Aldenmere Arts Centre hosts 200+ performances per year. Join the symphony orchestra, chamber choir, jazz ensemble, or one of six dramatic societies. All levels warmly welcomed.

Clubs & Societies

More than 180 student-led societies: academic, political, cultural, social, and entrepreneurial. The annual Societies Fair in October is a highlight of the freshers' week calendar.

Student Union

AUSU (Aldenmere University Students' Union) operates three bars, a café, a food bank, a nightclub, and advocacy services. Elected sabbatical officers represent students at every level of University governance.

Entrepreneurship Hub

The Aldenmere Entrepreneurship Hub offers seed funding (up to £25,000), mentorship from 80+ alumni founders, co-working space, and a structured 12-week startup accelerator each spring.

International Students

A Truly Global
Community

38% of Aldenmere students come from outside the UK — representing every continent and over 160 nations. Our International Student Advisory Service provides visa guidance, pre-arrival support, and an orientation programme designed to help students settle in quickly and thrive.

Student Support

We're Here
For You

Aldenmere takes student wellbeing seriously. Every student has a personal tutor, and a comprehensive network of professional services is available on campus and online — confidential, free of charge.

Student Support Portal
Counselling & Mental Health
Free individual counselling (up to 12 sessions per year), group therapy, and crisis support via a 24/7 helpline. Same-week appointments guaranteed for urgent needs.
Health Centre
On-campus NHS-registered GP practice, nursing team, and sexual health clinic. All registered Aldenmere students are automatically enrolled. Extended evening and weekend hours.
Disability & Inclusion
Specialist advisers support students with physical, sensory, mental health, and neurodiverse needs. Reasonable adjustments, note-taking support, and Disabled Students' Allowance guidance.
Financial Aid & Hardship Fund
Emergency grants of up to £2,500 for students facing unexpected financial hardship. Long-term bursaries, scholarship top-ups, and free access to the Campus Food Bank.
Careers & Employability
One-to-one career coaching, CV workshops, employer fairs (50+ companies attending the annual Law & Finance Fair alone), and alumni mentoring. Lifelong access after graduation.
Safety & Security
24-hour campus security, a dedicated Report & Support platform for disclosures of harassment or misconduct, and a fully staffed Student Safety Office.
