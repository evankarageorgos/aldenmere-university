LLB Hons · 3 Years · 360 Credits
Constitutional & Administrative Law

This programme offers a comprehensive grounding in public law, developing the analytical, research, and advocacy skills required for practice, policy-making, and legal scholarship.

Learning Outcomes
  • Analyse the constitutional principles underpinning UK, EU, and comparative legal systems.
  • Critically evaluate the role of judicial review in controlling executive power.
  • Apply statutory interpretation to complex factual scenarios.
  • Construct cogent oral and written legal arguments under time pressure.
  • Engage with primary sources including Acts of Parliament, case law, and treaty texts.

MBBS · 5 Years · GMC Accredited
Medicine (MBBS)

A five-year integrated programme in which biomedical science and early clinical exposure are woven together from Year 1. Years 4–5 are clinical, based at Aldenmere Teaching Hospital and partner NHS Trusts.

Learning Outcomes
  • Demonstrate a comprehensive understanding of human anatomy, physiology, and pathology.
  • Perform a systematic clinical history and physical examination across all major specialties.
  • Apply evidence-based medicine principles to diagnostic and therapeutic decision-making.
  • Communicate effectively with patients, families, and multidisciplinary teams.
  • Reflect critically on ethical dilemmas in clinical practice.

International Human Rights Law — LLM

Full syllabus available upon request. This programme covers international treaty regimes, UN mechanisms, regional human rights courts, and a 15,000-word dissertation. Contact the Admissions Office for the detailed course handbook.

Doctoral Research in Law — PhD

Candidates develop an original thesis under dual supervision. Research training modules, research ethics, and annual progression reviews are required. Please contact the Graduate Admissions team to discuss a prospective supervisor.

Molecular Oncology — MSc

Core modules: Cancer Genomics, Drug Discovery & Development, Immunotherapy, Clinical Trial Design. Research project (60 credits) in the Aldenmere Cancer Research Institute.

Structural & Civil Engineering — MSc

Modules: Advanced Structural Analysis, Geotechnics, Sustainable Infrastructure, BIM & Digital Engineering, Major Design Project. ICE accredited for Chartered Engineer status.

History (Medieval & Early Modern) — BA Hons

Year 1: Medieval Europe 500–1300; Year 2: The Later Middle Ages & Reformation (with Latin source reading); Year 3: Dissertation (10,000 words) and two advanced special subjects. Regular visits to the Special Collections archive.

Philosophy & Ethics — MA

Modules: Metaethics, Political Philosophy, Philosophy of Mind, Applied Ethics (Bioethics / AI / Global Justice). 15,000-word dissertation with a dedicated supervisor from the Philosophy Department.

Economics & Finance — BSc Hons

Year 1: Micro/Macro Foundations, Quantitative Methods. Year 2: Econometrics, Corporate Finance, International Trade. Year 3: Financial Markets Elective, Applied Research Project or Dissertation.

Master of Business Administration — MBA

Core: Strategy, Leadership, Finance, Operations, Marketing. Electives (choose 3 from 12): FinTech, Social Enterprise, Healthcare Management, Entrepreneurship, etc. Live consulting project with a FTSE 100 partner company.

Physics with Quantum Technologies — MPhys

Year 1–2: Classical Mechanics, Electromagnetism, Quantum Mechanics, Statistical Physics. Year 3–4: Quantum Information Theory, Photonics, Advanced Lab, MPhys Research Project in the Quantum Technologies Laboratory.
